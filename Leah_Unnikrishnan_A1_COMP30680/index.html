Did you know?

  1. The name 'Dublin' comes from an Old Irish Gaelic phrase 'Dubh linn' that translates to “Black Pool”
  2. Guinness is brewed in Ireland, and will continue be for many years to come…
  3. Dublin is home to over 770 pubs
  4. Dublin was recognised as an UNESCO city of literature in 2010
  5. Dublin's trams are called Luas, which is Gaelic for “speed.”
  6. Dublin has a pub older than America: The Brazen Head, dating back to 1198!
  7. Dublin was the first city to be named a UNESCO City of Literature.
  8. Dublin has over 1.2 million residents in the metropolitan area.
